# Store Listing Copy

## Product name

EatLog

## Short description

Track meals, snacks, and time gaps without an account.

## Full description

EatLog is a lightweight meal tracker focused on one core flow: log what you ate, when you ate, and keep an eye on snack frequency and meal intervals. The app works locally on your device, supports offline launch after the first online open, and keeps the interface compact for mobile use.

## Key points

- Log meals and snacks in a few taps
- Review day-by-day meal interval history
- Set daily goals for meals, snacks, and meal gaps
- Export local history from the device
- Works as a PWA after initial online open
